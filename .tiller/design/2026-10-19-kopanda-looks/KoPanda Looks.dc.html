3Wallpaper looks: 3a Plates · 3b Frosted Plates
Built for any image, tested on the busiest one: your flowers. There's no opacity or blur, so legibility comes from opaque plates, never from dimming the picture. The wallpaper shows in the margins and gutters only.
On the Paperwhite board the image is shown in greyscale, as the device will show it. The plates don't change.
2Playful looks: 2a Sticker Book · 2b Funnies · 2c Toybox
Same screens, same rules, more fun. Play only goes where it doesn't cost legibility: the clock, titles and the nav never tilt or sit on texture, and covers keep their real shape inside the frame.
Each look adds one texture or frame that does a job: die-cut rings that separate tiles on a dotted backing, Ben-Day halftone that becomes a real e-ink grey on the Paperwhite, and a solid block edge that shows a tile is pressable.
